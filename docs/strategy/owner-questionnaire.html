One thing to know

Every image on the prototypes right now is an AI-generated conceptual rendering — the buildings, the interiors, the parking deck, the pool. None of them are your jobs. They exist to show how the site behaves before anyone picks up a camera, and they're labelled as renderings throughout.

How many get replaced with real work is the choice you made above. Our recommendation: real photographs for anything you call a project, generated imagery only for concepts and atmosphere. Someone deciding whether to hand you a buildout can tell the difference — and the whole argument of this site is that you actually did the work.
