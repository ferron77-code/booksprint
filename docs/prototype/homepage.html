Landscape lighting care · Launching

Lights fail slowly,
then all at once

Join the program waitlist

Fixtures are built to last. Landscapers, root growth, shifting soil and three-year lamp life are what actually kill a system. Every quarter we come out and put it back to the day it was installed.

Q1

Clean lenses, inspect every fixture, check transformer load and timer programming.

Q2

Clear foliage growth, reposition fixtures that have shifted or been buried.

Q3

Re-bury wiring exposed by settling ground, test connections, replace failing lamps.

Q4

Full system troubleshoot and a written condition report before the season.
