Read this before showing it to anyone

Every frame here is drawn live in code — geometry and light computed in the browser, in real time. There is no photography on this page, no stock footage, and no AI-generated imagery. Nothing on screen depicts a real Worldwide Distributors project, and nothing should be presented as one.

This is a demonstration of the mechanic and the feel: the forward vector, the transformation, the light behaviour, the pacing of the copy against the movement. In production the same structure carries real footage of real jobs.

What survives to production

The structure is the deliverable, and it does not change when the footage arrives:

  • One unbroken forward camera vector
  • Six beats, each tied to a capability
  • Raw at the start, finished and lit at the end
  • Type that holds still while the world moves
  • Chapter and depth readout as the only chrome

What a real shoot would replace

The drawn corridor stands in for footage that has to be filmed:

  • A pallet breaking open in the warehouse
  • A lighting plan on the design table
  • Loading the truck
  • Conduit, panels and fixtures going in
  • Framing, drywall, finishes
  • The same room at night, fully lit
